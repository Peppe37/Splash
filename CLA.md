# Contributor License Agreement

> **Draft placeholder.** This text is not final and has not been reviewed by a lawyer. It marks the intent so the process is ready when the repository goes public. Do not accept contributions until the final text is in place.

## Why a CLA

Splash is released under the [GPL-3.0](LICENSE). The maintainer may also want to distribute it under other terms in the future, for example in the Mac App Store, or with paid features. The GPL does not allow that for code written by other people unless they agree to it. A CLA lets contributors keep their copyright while granting the maintainer that right.

## Intent of the final text

By contributing, you:

1. **Keep your copyright** in your contribution.
2. **Grant the project owner** a perpetual, worldwide, non-exclusive, royalty-free, irrevocable license to use, modify, sublicense and distribute your contribution, **under the GPL-3.0 and under any other license** the owner chooses, including proprietary ones.
3. **Confirm** that you wrote the contribution (or have the right to submit it), and that it does not include code under a license that is incompatible with the above.
4. **Understand** that the project remains available to everyone under the GPL-3.0, including your contribution as published.

## How it will work

- A bot comment on the first pull request asks the contributor to agree by replying.
- The agreement is recorded in the repository.
- Dependencies must stay permissive (MIT, BSD, Apache-2.0) so the owner can relicense.
